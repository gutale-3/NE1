What the installation covers

We do the whole job, not part of it. Either have all the equipment ready on site, or buy everything from us and we handle it from start to finish.

  • Mounting the cameras, indoors and outdoors
  • Wiring to every camera, with trunking when supplied
  • Connecting the cameras to the DVR/NVR
  • Mounting the TV and connecting the recorder to it
  • Connecting everything to power
  • Access control, video intercom and networking installs
  • Phone viewing over Wi-Fi, and how to share or remove access for others
  • Everything left working, and shown to you, before we leave

Installation questions

How much does CCTV installation cost in Nairobi?

NE charges KES 1,500 to 3,000 per camera in Nairobi. A house with simple cable runs is about KES 1,500 per camera; larger sites with more cabling or work at height are up to KES 3,000 per camera.

Do you install CCTV outside Nairobi?

Yes. Outside Nairobi the price depends on the location and the work, charged by time or per job as agreed. The client covers the team's transport and accommodation.

Is the equipment included in the installation price?

No. The installation price covers labour. Cameras, recorders, hard disks and cabling are bought separately, for example as one of NE's ready-made bundles.

Do you also install access control, intercoms and networking?

Yes. NE installs access control, video intercoms and networking products as well as CCTV, and finishes the whole job: mounting, wiring, trunking when supplied, connecting the recorder and TV, and power.

Do you set up viewing on my phone?

Yes. As long as there is Wi-Fi or internet on site, we add the cameras to your phone and show you how to share access with other people and remove it.

How much does a password reset cost?

A DVR password reset is KES 2,000 for any size. An NVR reset is KES 3,000 up to 16 channels and KES 5,000 above 16 channels. Bring the recorder to the NE showroom at BBS Mall, Monday to Saturday; it usually takes one to two hours. Resetting a forgotten Hik-Connect app password is free: we guide you through it.

What do I need to have ready before installation?

All products must be on site before the job starts, so the team can work without stopping.

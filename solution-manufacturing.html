FAQ

Common questions about Manufacturing security

What perimeter protection does Hikvision offer for factories?
A three-tier system — optical cameras under 50m, radar-camera linkage under 200m, and thermal cameras beyond 200m — covering the full range around a factory site.
Can Hikvision manage visitor and vehicle access at a manufacturing plant?
Yes. Visitor management, access control of key areas, and car or truck entrance-and-exit management are all part of the personnel and vehicle management toolkit.
What is Hikvision AR workshop inspection?
An augmented-reality tool that overlays equipment information onto a live camera feed, helping staff share workshop information and catch maintenance issues faster.
How can NE help protect a factory's perimeter?
NE designs tiered perimeter coverage — standard cameras for close-range detail, radar-camera linkage for mid-range detection, and thermal cameras for long-range or low-visibility coverage — matched to the size and layout of the site.

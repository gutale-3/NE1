FAQ

Common questions about NE's solutions

Is NE an authorized Hikvision distributor?
Yes. NE sources every product directly through Hikvision's official distribution channel, so what you buy is genuine hardware, not grey-market stock.
Does NE install what it sells?
Yes. NE's own technicians handle site surveys, installation, system design and after-sales support — one point of contact from purchase to a working system.
I'm not sure which cameras or system I need — can NE help?
Yes. Tell NE about your site, industry and budget on WhatsApp or through the contact page, and NE will recommend the right cameras, recorders and access control for your scenario.
Does NE offer bulk or wholesale pricing?
Yes. NE offers volume pricing for installers, integrators and contractors sourcing Hikvision equipment for multiple sites or ongoing projects.
How do I get a quote from NE?

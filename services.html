FAQ

Common questions about installation & support

Does NE provide professional installation for Hikvision systems, or can I DIY?
NE offers full professional installation — cabling, mounting, NVR/DVR configuration and network setup — for both small single-camera jobs and full multi-building deployments. Basic single-camera add-ons are DIY-friendly if you already have an existing system; anything with new cable runs or multiple cameras is worth having installed professionally to avoid rework.
Can NE install IP-based access control systems?
Yes — NE installs and configures IP-based access control including card/fob readers, face recognition terminals and door controllers, and can integrate them with an existing CCTV or video intercom system.

FAQ

Common questions about Energy security

How does Hikvision protect a solar farm with no on-site staff?
Thermal cameras detect intrusions day or night in any weather, and radar-camera linkage automatically points nearby PTZ cameras at a detected threat.
Can solar farm security cameras run without mains power?
Yes. Hikvision's solar-powered cameras remove the need for additional wiring at remote sites.
What does remote inspection look like for a solar farm?
A HikCentral video-wall dashboard gives the operations team a bird's-eye view of the whole site, cutting the need for physical site visits.
ColorVu or thermal/infrared — what's best for solar farm perimeter security?
For unlit, unstaffed solar sites, thermal cameras are usually the better fit — they detect intrusions by heat signature in total darkness, fog or dust, well beyond ColorVu's effective range. ColorVu is more useful at a site's access gate or control building, where colour identification of a person or vehicle matters.
Does NE offer thermal cameras for perimeter protection at energy sites?
Yes — thermal cameras paired with radar-camera linkage automatically detect and track intrusions along a solar farm or substation perimeter, alerting operators without needing on-site staff.

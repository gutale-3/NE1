FAQ

Common questions about Small Business security

What's included in a Hikvision small business security kit?
Typically an NVR, cameras, access control and an alarm system sized to the site — matched to a store, office or home rather than an enterprise deployment.
Can I monitor my shop or home remotely with Hikvision?
Yes, via the Hik-Connect app, which supports both personal (single site) and team (multi-site) management modes.
Does Hikvision offer video intercom for small offices?
Yes — indoor and door stations with two-way audio and video secure front gates and office entrances.
How do I choose CCTV cameras for a shop or restaurant?
Prioritise coverage of entrances, the till or cash point, stockroom and any blind corners, and choose ColorVu or Smart Hybrid Light cameras for colour identification during business hours and after closing. Restaurants often add discreet indoor domes for the dining area and kitchen pass, while shops usually add a wide-angle camera over the main floor.
Is a commercial CCTV system different from a home setup?
Not fundamentally — the same Hikvision hardware covers both — but commercial systems are usually specified for longer storage retention for dispute or insurance purposes, more camera channels, and integration with access control or an alarm system, where a home setup is often just a handful of cameras and an app.

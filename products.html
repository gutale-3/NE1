What's the best IP camera brand to buy in Kenya?
Hikvision is the IP camera brand NE stocks and backs with local warranty support — it's the world's largest CCTV manufacturer, with the parts and firmware support network in Kenya to back it up, which matters more long-term than any single spec comparison.
What's the best Hikvision ColorVu camera for outdoor use?
For outdoor coverage, look at ColorVu bullet or turret cameras rated IP66/IP67 with a wide-angle lens and a supplemental white light range matched to the area you're covering — NE can recommend the exact model once we know the mounting height and distance to cover.
Can NE help design a complete CCTV system layout for my property?
Yes — NE's technicians will walk the site (or review your floor plan), recommend camera placement and count, and produce an itemised quote covering cameras, NVR/DVR, cabling and installation.

Nashnaal Electronics (NE) — Hikvision Distributor in Kenya

An authorized Hikvision distributor and retailer, based in Nairobi, Kenya.

NE supplies genuine CCTV cameras, IP cameras, NVRs, access control, video intercom and public address systems to installers, businesses and homeowners across Kenya.

Every product NE sells is sourced directly through Hikvision's official distribution channel — so what you buy is exactly what gets installed and serviced, with no grey-market guesswork.

Beyond supply, NE's own team handles installation, system design and after-sales support, giving customers one point of contact from purchase through to a working, maintained system.
